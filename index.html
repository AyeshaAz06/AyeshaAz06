About Me

I am a Senior Technical Writer and Content Strategist with over 20 years of experience building documentation systems (not just documents) for enterprise software, cybersecurity, and identity management products.

One of my most ambitious professional contribution was architecting a full migration of product documentation from MadCap Flare to a VS Code + Markdown + Docusaurus stack in under two months at Imanami/Netwrix. This wasn't a tool switch. It was a shift to docs-as-code, with automated publishing, Azure DevOps to GitHub version control, and a contribution model for globally distributed SME teams.

I specialize in the cybersecurity and identity management domain, where precision matters. A misunderstood configuration step in a security product isn't just adocumentation failure; it's a risk. I bring both the technical depth and the editorial discipline this niche demands.

Since September 2025, I work as an independent cybersecurity and compliance writer, producing SEO-optimized long-form content on identity security, data governance, risk management, and regulatory compliance. I am adept at using AI-assisted research workflows with rigorous editorial validation.
